# Image Prompts — Ratios: The Basics

**Mission id:** `ratios-intro-v1`
**Image tool:** Gemini
**Style guidelines:** Dark background friendly (the app is dark-themed).
Aim for high contrast on light-colored key elements. Clean geometric shapes,
flat illustration or cinematic photorealism as noted per slide. Avoid text in
images — labels come from the app. When numbers must appear in an image
(e.g., a table), keep them legible and minimal.

---

## s1-hook — Fruit punch scenario

**File to save to:** `public/mission-images/ratios-intro-v1/s1-hook.png`
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (roughly 1.78)
**Prompt for Gemini:**

> A glass pitcher of bright red fruit punch on a dark wooden table, viewed
> from a three-quarter angle. Next to the pitcher, two clear glass measuring

> slightly cinematic lighting from the upper left. Condensation on the
> glass. Dark, uncluttered background so the colors of the drinks pop. No
> labels, no text anywhere in the image. 16:9 landscape composition.

**Alt text (for the JSON `alt` field):**

> A pitcher of fruit punch next to two measuring cups labeled juice and soda.
